Privacy Policy - IsaGrade

Last updated: 23 September 2026

What IsaGrade does

IsaGrade is a browser extension that calculates, directly in your browser, the minimum grade you need on appls.edufr.ch/isaweb to maintain your current grade point average or raise it by 0.5.

What we collect

IsaGrade stores your chosen display preferences (gradient colors and interface language) locally on your device, using the browser's built-in storage API. This is only used to remember your settings between sessions.

What we do NOT collect

IsaGrade's content script reads the grade table displayed on appls.edufr.ch/isaweb solely to perform the grade calculation locally, in your browser. This grade data is never stored, never transmitted, and never sent to any server. IsaGrade has no backend, no analytics, and no third-party integrations.

Where data is processed

Entirely on your device, within your browser. No data leaves your computer at any point.

Data retention and deletion

Your stored display preferences remain until you uninstall the extension or manually clear them via your browser's extension storage settings (chrome://extensions). There is no other data to retain or delete, as no other data is collected.

Third parties

IsaGrade does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. It does not use data for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending purposes.
